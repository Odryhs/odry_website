Creative statement

I am an aspiring artist. Although I haven’t settled on what exactly I want to focus on, I know I mainly want to do digital art. Such as making 3d models and animation. My motivation for this is that I like the satisfaction of seeing the final form of something. For example in blender I can start off with a sphere and end up turning it into a flower. I have always enjoyed art class in school. It was one of the classes I looked forward to and I was sad whenever I didn’t end up getting art class.
